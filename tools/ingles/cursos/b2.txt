# ===================== B2 · SEMANA 1: CONDICIONALES =====================

=== eng-b2-w1 | 💭 | B2 · Semana 1 · Clase 1: Second Conditional (situaciones imaginarias)
desc: If I had more time, I would learn Japanese. If I were you, I'd… What would you do if…? Comparación con el First Conditional.
- ⏳ | If I had more time, I would learn Japanese | Si tuviera más tiempo, aprendería japonés | If I had more time, I would learn Japanese. | Si tuviera más tiempo, aprendería japonés.
- 💰 | If I won the lottery, I'd travel the world | Si ganara la lotería, viajaría por el mundo | If I won the lottery, I'd travel the world. | Si ganara la lotería, viajaría por el mundo.
- 🧑‍🏫 | If I were you, I'd talk to her | Yo que tú, hablaría con ella | If I were you, I'd talk to her. | Yo que tú, hablaría con ella.
- ❓ | What would you do if you saw a ghost? | ¿Qué harías si vieras un fantasma? | What would you do if you saw a ghost? | ¿Qué harías si vieras un fantasma?
- 🦸 | If I could fly, I'd go to school by air | Si pudiera volar, iría al colegio por aire | If I could fly, I'd go to school by air. | Si pudiera volar, iría al colegio por aire.
- 🏠 | If we lived near the beach, we'd swim every day | Si viviéramos cerca de la playa, nadaríamos a diario | If we lived near the beach, we'd swim every day. | Si viviéramos cerca de la playa, nadaríamos todos los días.
- 🚫 | I wouldn't do that if I were you | Yo no haría eso si fuera tú | I wouldn't do that if I were you. | Yo no haría eso si fuera tú.
- 🗣️ | If she spoke English, she could get the job | Si hablara inglés, podría conseguir el trabajo | If she spoke English, she could get the job. | Si hablara inglés, podría conseguir el trabajo.
- 🐶 | If I didn't have a dog, I'd feel lonely | Si no tuviera perro, me sentiría solo | If I didn't have a dog, I'd feel lonely. | Si no tuviera perro, me sentiría solo.
- 🌍 | If you could live anywhere, where would you live? | Si pudieras vivir en cualquier lugar, ¿dónde vivirías? | If you could live anywhere, where would you live? | Si pudieras vivir en cualquier lugar, ¿dónde vivirías?
- 🔀 | If it rains, I'll stay home (real) | Si llueve, me quedaré en casa (real) | If it rains tomorrow, I'll stay home. | Si llueve mañana, me quedaré en casa.
- 🌈 | If it snowed in Lima, I'd be amazed (imaginario) | Si nevara en Lima, me asombraría (imaginario) | If it snowed in Lima, I'd be amazed. | Si nevara en Lima, me asombraría.
teoria:
intro: El Second Conditional habla de situaciones imaginarias, poco probables o contrarias a la realidad en el presente o futuro. Es como el «si + subjuntivo, condicional» del español: si tuviera, haría.
tabla: La fórmula ; Parte | Estructura | Ejemplo*
| Condición | If + pasado simple | If I had a car,
| Resultado | would / could / might + verbo | I would drive to Cusco.
| Negativa | If + didn't…, wouldn't… | If I didn't study, I wouldn't pass.
| Pregunta | What would you do if…? | What would you do if you were rich?
tabla: Todas las personas ; Persona | Ejemplo*
| I | If I were rich, I'd buy a house.
| You | If you were rich, you'd buy a house.
| He / She | If she were rich, she'd buy a house.
| We | If we were rich, we'd buy a house.
| They | If they were rich, they'd buy a house.
tabla: First vs Second ; First* (real, posible) | Second* (imaginario, improbable)
| If I have time, I'll help you. | If I had time, I'd help you.
| If it's sunny, we'll go out. | If it were sunny, we'd go out.
| If she calls, I'll answer. | If she called, I'd answer.
ej: If I were the president, I would build more schools. | Si yo fuera presidente, construiría más colegios.
ej: Would you help me if I asked you? — Of course I would. | ¿Me ayudarías si te lo pidiera? — Claro que sí.
ojo: Nunca pongas «would» después de if: If I would have ✗ → If I had ✓.
ojo: Con «be» se prefiere «were» para todas las personas: If I were you ✓ (If I was you es informal).
ojo: El pasado aquí NO es pasado de tiempo: «If I had» habla del presente imaginario.
pron: I'd suena «áid», you'd «iúd», wouldn't «úudent» (la l no suena).

=== eng-b2-w1-c2 | 📊 | B2 · Semana 1 · Clase 2: Todos los condicionales (0, 1, 2, 3 y mixtos)
desc: Tabla completa: zero, first, second, third y mixed conditionals con ejemplos, comas y variaciones (unless, provided that, as long as).
- 🧊 | If you heat ice, it melts | Si calientas hielo, se derrite | If you heat ice, it melts. | Si calientas hielo, se derrite.
- 🌧️ | If it rains, we'll cancel the trip | Si llueve, cancelaremos el viaje | If it rains, we'll cancel the trip. | Si llueve, cancelaremos el viaje.
- 💸 | If I had money, I'd buy a bike | Si tuviera dinero, compraría una bici | If I had money, I'd buy a bike. | Si tuviera dinero, compraría una bici.
- 📚 | If I had studied, I would have passed | Si hubiera estudiado, habría aprobado | If I had studied, I would have passed the exam. | Si hubiera estudiado, habría aprobado el examen.
- 🚌 | If I hadn't missed the bus, I wouldn't have been late | Si no hubiera perdido el bus, no habría llegado tarde | If I hadn't missed the bus, I wouldn't have been late. | Si no hubiera perdido el bus, no habría llegado tarde.
- 🔀 | If I had slept more, I wouldn't be tired now | Si hubiera dormido más, no estaría cansado ahora | If I had slept more last night, I wouldn't be tired now. | Si hubiera dormido más anoche, no estaría cansado ahora.
- 🎓 | If I were braver, I would have said something | Si fuera más valiente, habría dicho algo | If I were braver, I would have said something. | Si fuera más valiente, habría dicho algo.
- ✅ | As long as you're careful, you can go | Siempre que tengas cuidado, puedes ir | As long as you're careful, you can go. | Siempre que tengas cuidado, puedes ir.
- 📝 | Provided that you finish, you can leave | Con tal de que termines, puedes irte | Provided that you finish your work, you can leave early. | Con tal de que termines tu trabajo, puedes irte temprano.
- ⛔ | Unless you apologize, she won't forgive you | Si no pides perdón, no te perdonará | Unless you apologize, she won't forgive you. | Si no pides perdón, no te perdonará.
- ☂️ | Take an umbrella in case it rains | Lleva paraguas por si llueve | Take an umbrella in case it rains. | Lleva paraguas por si llueve.
- 🤔 | Suppose you lost your phone, what would you do? | Supón que perdieras tu celular, ¿qué harías? | Suppose you lost your phone, what would you do? | Supón que perdieras tu celular, ¿qué harías?
teoria:
intro: Aquí está el mapa completo de los condicionales en inglés. Cada uno combina tiempos verbales distintos según si hablamos de algo real, posible, imaginario o del pasado que ya no se puede cambiar.
tabla: Los condicionales ; Tipo | Condición (if…) | Resultado | Ejemplo*
| Zero | presente | presente | If you mix red and blue, you get purple.
| First | presente | will + verbo | If you study, you'll pass.
| Second | pasado simple | would + verbo | If I had wings, I'd fly.
| Third | past perfect (had + participio) | would have + participio | If I had known, I would have helped.
| Mixto 3→2 | past perfect | would + verbo (ahora) | If I had taken the job, I'd be rich now.
| Mixto 2→3 | pasado simple | would have + participio | If I spoke French, I'd have understood him.
tabla: Otras palabras condicionales ; Inglés* | Español | Ejemplo*
| unless | a menos que / si no | Unless you hurry, you'll be late.
| as long as | siempre que | You can stay as long as you're quiet.
| provided (that) | con tal de que | I'll lend it provided you return it.
| in case | por si | Take a jacket in case it gets cold.
| suppose / supposing | supón que | Suppose you won, what would you buy?
| otherwise | si no, de lo contrario | Hurry, otherwise we'll miss it.
ej: If I hadn't gone to that party, I wouldn't have met my best friend. | Si no hubiera ido a esa fiesta, no habría conocido a mi mejor amigo.
ej: If you need help, call me. | Si necesitas ayuda, llámame.
ojo: Nunca «would» en la parte del if: If I would have known ✗ → If I had known ✓.
ojo: «In case» no es «if»: Take an umbrella in case it rains = llévalo por si acaso (antes de saber si llueve).
ojo: Coma solo cuando el if va primero: If it rains, I'll stay. / I'll stay if it rains.
pron: Would have suena «údav» o «úda». Hadn't suena «jádent».

=== eng-b2-w1-c3 | 😔 | B2 · Semana 1 · Clase 3: Third Conditional, I wish, If only
desc: If I had known, I would have come. I wish I had more time. I wish I hadn't said that. If only I could fly. I wish you would stop.
- 📞 | If I had known, I would have called you | Si lo hubiera sabido, te habría llamado | If I had known you were sick, I would have called you. | Si hubiera sabido que estabas enfermo, te habría llamado.
- 🏆 | We would have won if we had trained more | Habríamos ganado si hubiéramos entrenado más | We would have won if we had trained more. | Habríamos ganado si hubiéramos entrenado más.
- ⏳ | I wish I had more time | Ojalá tuviera más tiempo | I wish I had more time to read. | Ojalá tuviera más tiempo para leer.
- 🗣️ | I wish I hadn't said that | Ojalá no hubiera dicho eso | I wish I hadn't said that to my friend. | Ojalá no le hubiera dicho eso a mi amigo.
- 🦅 | If only I could fly! | ¡Si tan solo pudiera volar! | If only I could fly over the Andes! | ¡Si tan solo pudiera volar sobre los Andes!
- 🔇 | I wish you would stop talking | Ojalá dejaras de hablar | I wish you would stop talking during the movie. | Ojalá dejaras de hablar durante la película.
- 🏖️ | I wish I were on the beach now | Ojalá estuviera en la playa ahora | I wish I were on the beach right now. | Ojalá estuviera en la playa ahora mismo.
- 🎒 | If only I had brought my jacket | Si tan solo hubiera traído mi casaca | If only I had brought my jacket! It's freezing. | ¡Si tan solo hubiera traído mi casaca! Hace un frío terrible.
- 🧠 | She wishes she spoke Quechua | Ella quisiera hablar quechua | She wishes she spoke Quechua like her grandma. | Ella quisiera hablar quechua como su abuela.
- 🚗 | It's time we left | Ya es hora de que nos vayamos | It's late. It's time we left. | Es tarde. Ya es hora de que nos vayamos.
- 💡 | I'd rather you didn't tell anyone | Preferiría que no le dijeras a nadie | I'd rather you didn't tell anyone about this. | Preferiría que no le dijeras a nadie sobre esto.
- ⚠️ | You should have told me | Debiste decírmelo | You should have told me earlier! | ¡Debiste decírmelo antes!
teoria:
intro: El Third Conditional imagina un pasado diferente que ya no se puede cambiar. Para deseos y arrepentimientos usamos I wish / If only, con tiempos que «retroceden» como en los condicionales.
tabla: Third Conditional ; Parte | Estructura | Ejemplo*
| Condición | If + had + participio | If I had left earlier,
| Resultado | would have + participio | I would have caught the train.
| Negativa | If I hadn't…, I wouldn't have… | If she hadn't helped me, I wouldn't have finished.
| Pregunta | What would you have done if…? | What would you have done if you had lost?
tabla: Deseos y arrepentimientos ; Deseo sobre | Estructura | Ejemplo*
| el presente | wish + pasado simple | I wish I lived in Cusco.
| el presente (be) | wish + were | I wish I were taller.
| el pasado | wish + past perfect | I wish I had studied more.
| una habilidad | wish + could | I wish I could sing.
| algo molesto (de otro) | wish + would | I wish you would listen to me.
| énfasis | If only + igual que wish | If only I had known!
tabla: Más estructuras irreales ; Inglés* | Español
| It's time we went. | Ya es hora de que nos vayamos.
| I'd rather you stayed. | Preferiría que te quedaras.
| You should have called. | Debiste llamar.
| You shouldn't have shouted. | No debiste gritar.
ej: I wish I hadn't eaten so much. Now I feel sick. | Ojalá no hubiera comido tanto. Ahora me siento mal.
ej: If you had asked me, I would have helped you. | Si me hubieras pedido, te habría ayudado.
ojo: «Ojalá» sobre el futuro se dice con hope: I hope it doesn't rain tomorrow ✓ (no I wish it doesn't rain).
ojo: No uses «would» para desearte algo a ti mismo: I wish I would be rich ✗ → I wish I were rich ✓.
pron: «I would have» se reduce a «ái wúdəv» o «aid'ev». «Should have» suena «shúdəv» (nunca «should of» al escribir).

# ===================== B2 · SEMANA 2: PRESENT PERFECT CONTINUOUS =====================

=== eng-b2-w2 | ⌛ | B2 · Semana 2 · Clase 1: Present Perfect Continuous
desc: I've been studying for three hours. She's been working here since 2019. How long have you been waiting? You look tired. Have you been running?
- 📚 | I've been studying for three hours | Llevo tres horas estudiando | I've been studying for three hours. I need a break. | Llevo tres horas estudiando. Necesito un descanso.
- 🏢 | She's been working here since 2019 | Trabaja aquí desde 2019 | She's been working here since 2019. | Ella trabaja aquí desde 2019.
- ⏱️ | How long have you been waiting? | ¿Cuánto tiempo llevas esperando? | How long have you been waiting? — For twenty minutes. | ¿Cuánto llevas esperando? — Veinte minutos.
- 🥵 | Have you been running? | ¿Has estado corriendo? | You're out of breath. Have you been running? | Estás sin aliento. ¿Has estado corriendo?
- 🌧️ | It's been raining all day | Ha estado lloviendo todo el día | It's been raining all day. | Ha estado lloviendo todo el día.
- 😢 | Why have you been crying? | ¿Por qué has estado llorando? | Your eyes are red. Why have you been crying? | Tienes los ojos rojos. ¿Por qué has estado llorando?
- 🎸 | I've been learning the guitar lately | Últimamente estoy aprendiendo guitarra | I've been learning the guitar lately. | Últimamente he estado aprendiendo guitarra.
- 😴 | I haven't been sleeping well | No he estado durmiendo bien | I haven't been sleeping well recently. | Últimamente no he estado durmiendo bien.
- 🎨 | They've been painting the house | Han estado pintando la casa | They've been painting the house all week. | Han estado pintando la casa toda la semana.
- 🤔 | What have you been doing? | ¿Qué has estado haciendo? | What have you been doing? Your hands are dirty! | ¿Qué has estado haciendo? ¡Tienes las manos sucias!
- 🧘 | I've been feeling much better | Me he estado sintiendo mucho mejor | Since I started yoga, I've been feeling much better. | Desde que empecé yoga, me he sentido mucho mejor.
teoria:
intro: El Present Perfect Continuous habla de una acción que empezó en el pasado y sigue (o acaba de terminar), poniendo énfasis en la DURACIÓN o en el resultado visible. En español: «llevo… -ando», «he estado… -ando».
tabla: La fórmula ; Persona | Afirmativa* | Negativa* | Pregunta*
| I / You / We / They | I've been working. | I haven't been working. | Have you been working?
| He / She / It | She's been working. | She hasn't been working. | Has she been working?
tabla: Usos ; Uso | Ejemplo*
| Duración hasta ahora (for / since) | I've been living here for ten years.
| Pregunta por la duración | How long have you been learning English?
| Resultado visible de una acción reciente | You're wet. Have you been swimming?
| Situación temporal reciente (lately, recently) | I've been working a lot lately.
tabla: Palabras clave ; Inglés* | Español
| for two hours / since Monday | durante dos horas / desde el lunes
| all day / all week | todo el día / toda la semana
| lately / recently | últimamente / recientemente
| How long…? | ¿Cuánto tiempo…?
ej: I've been waiting for you since five o'clock! | ¡Te he estado esperando desde las cinco!
ej: She looks tired because she's been working all night. | Se ve cansada porque ha estado trabajando toda la noche.
ojo: «Llevo dos años estudiando inglés» = I've been studying English for two years (no «I carry two years…» ni «I study English for two years»).
ojo: Los verbos de estado no van en continuo: I've been knowing her ✗ → I've known her ✓.
pron: «I've been» suena «aivbin», muy rápido. «She's been» suena «shísbin».

=== eng-b2-w2-c2 | ⚖️ | B2 · Semana 2 · Clase 2: Perfect simple vs Perfect continuous
desc: I've read three books (cantidad, resultado) vs I've been reading all morning (actividad, duración). Verbos de estado. Already, still, yet.
- 📗 | I've read three books this month | He leído tres libros este mes | I've read three books this month. | He leído tres libros este mes.
- 📖 | I've been reading all morning | He estado leyendo toda la mañana | I've been reading all morning. | He estado leyendo toda la mañana.
- 🎨 | I've painted the kitchen | He pintado la cocina (terminado) | I've painted the kitchen. Do you like it? | He pintado la cocina. ¿Te gusta?
- 🖌️ | I've been painting the kitchen | He estado pintando la cocina | I've been painting the kitchen. That's why I'm dirty. | He estado pintando la cocina. Por eso estoy sucio.
- ✉️ | She has written ten emails today | Ha escrito diez correos hoy | She has written ten emails today. | Ella ha escrito diez correos hoy.
- ⌨️ | She has been writing emails for hours | Lleva horas escribiendo correos | She has been writing emails for hours. | Lleva horas escribiendo correos.
- 🤝 | I've known him for years | Lo conozco desde hace años | I've known him for years. | Lo conozco desde hace años.
- 🏡 | We've had this car since 2015 | Tenemos este carro desde 2015 | We've had this car since 2015. | Tenemos este carro desde 2015.
- ⏳ | I still haven't finished | Todavía no he terminado | I still haven't finished my essay. | Todavía no he terminado mi ensayo.
- 🏃 | How many kilometers have you run? | ¿Cuántos kilómetros has corrido? | How many kilometers have you run today? | ¿Cuántos kilómetros has corrido hoy?
- ⏱️ | How long have you been running? | ¿Cuánto tiempo llevas corriendo? | How long have you been running? | ¿Cuánto tiempo llevas corriendo?
teoria:
intro: Los dos tiempos se parecen, pero el simple se fija en el RESULTADO o la cantidad (cuánto/cuántos) y el continuo en la ACTIVIDAD y su duración (cuánto tiempo).
tabla: Comparación ; Perfect Simple* (resultado) | Perfect Continuous* (actividad)
| I've written two chapters. | I've been writing all day.
| How many cakes have you made? | How long have you been baking?
| She has cleaned her room. (está limpio) | She has been cleaning. (está cansada)
| I've lived here for ten years. (permanente) | I've been living with my aunt this month. (temporal)
tabla: Verbos de estado (solo simple) ; Verbo* | Ejemplo*
| know | I've known her since school.
| have (= tener) | I've had this phone for a year.
| be | I've been here for an hour.
| like / love | I've always loved music.
| believe / understand | I've never understood it.
ej: I've been cooking all afternoon and I've made three dishes. | He estado cocinando toda la tarde y he hecho tres platos.
ej: How long have you known each other? | ¿Desde cuándo se conocen?
ojo: Con números o cantidades usa el simple: I've been reading three books ✗ → I've read three books ✓.
ojo: «Still» va antes del verbo en negativa: I still haven't finished ✓.
pron: Compara el ritmo: «aiv rítn» (I've written) vs «aiv bin ráiting» (I've been writing).

# ===================== B2 · SEMANA 3: CONECTORES AVANZADOS =====================

=== eng-b2-w3 | 🧷 | B2 · Semana 3 · Clase 1: Contraste (although, despite, however, whereas)
desc: Although it rained, we went. Despite the rain… In spite of being tired… However, nevertheless, whereas, while, even though.
- 🌧️ | Although it was raining, we went out | Aunque llovía, salimos | Although it was raining, we went out. | Aunque llovía, salimos.
- ☔ | Despite the rain, we went out | A pesar de la lluvia, salimos | Despite the rain, we went out. | A pesar de la lluvia, salimos.
- 😩 | In spite of being tired, she kept working | A pesar de estar cansada, siguió trabajando | In spite of being tired, she kept working. | A pesar de estar cansada, siguió trabajando.
- 🔄 | However, the results were disappointing | Sin embargo, los resultados fueron decepcionantes | We worked hard. However, the results were disappointing. | Trabajamos duro. Sin embargo, los resultados fueron decepcionantes.
- 🧱 | Nevertheless, we didn't give up | No obstante, no nos rendimos | It was difficult. Nevertheless, we didn't give up. | Fue difícil. No obstante, no nos rendimos.
- ↔️ | My brother is tall, whereas I'm short | Mi hermano es alto, mientras que yo soy bajo | My brother is tall, whereas I'm short. | Mi hermano es alto, mientras que yo soy bajo.
- 💪 | Even though he was sick, he came | Aunque estaba enfermo, vino | Even though he was sick, he came to the match. | Aunque estaba enfermo, vino al partido.
- 🧾 | Despite the fact that it was expensive, I bought it | A pesar de que era caro, lo compré | Despite the fact that it was expensive, I bought it. | A pesar de que era caro, lo compré.
- 🌓 | On the other hand, | Por otro lado, | City life is exciting. On the other hand, it's stressful. | La vida en la ciudad es emocionante. Por otro lado, es estresante.
- 🔁 | Instead of complaining, do something | En vez de quejarte, haz algo | Instead of complaining, do something about it. | En vez de quejarte, haz algo al respecto.
- 🎯 | Even so, | Aun así, | The hotel was old. Even so, we loved it. | El hotel era viejo. Aun así, nos encantó.
- 🆚 | Unlike my sister, I love spicy food | A diferencia de mi hermana, me encanta lo picante | Unlike my sister, I love spicy food. | A diferencia de mi hermana, me encanta la comida picante.
teoria:
intro: En B2 necesitamos variar los conectores de contraste. Lo importante es saber qué va DESPUÉS de cada uno: una oración completa, un sustantivo o un verbo en -ing.
tabla: ¿Qué va después? ; Conector* | Después va | Ejemplo*
| although / even though / though | oración (sujeto + verbo) | Although it was cold, we swam.
| despite / in spite of | sustantivo o -ing | Despite the cold, we swam. / Despite being cold…
| despite the fact that | oración | Despite the fact that it was cold, we swam.
| however / nevertheless / even so | nueva oración, con coma | It was cold. However, we swam.
| whereas / while | oración (compara dos cosas) | I like tea, whereas she prefers coffee.
| unlike | sustantivo | Unlike me, she's very calm.
| instead of | sustantivo o -ing | Instead of watching TV, read a book.
tabla: Grado de formalidad ; Informal | Neutro | Formal*
| but | however | nevertheless / nonetheless
| though (al final) | although | even though
| still | even so | notwithstanding
ej: Even though I studied a lot, I didn't pass. | Aunque estudié mucho, no aprobé.
ej: Some people love the city, whereas others prefer the countryside. | Algunos aman la ciudad, mientras que otros prefieren el campo.
ojo: Despite of ✗ → Despite ✓ / In spite of ✓.
ojo: Despite + oración ✗: Despite it was raining ✗ → Despite the rain / Although it was raining ✓.
ojo: However no une dos oraciones con coma: I was tired, however I went ✗ → I was tired. However, I went ✓ (o ; however,).
pron: Although suena «oldóu». Nevertheless suena «néverdeles». Whereas suena «uéras».

=== eng-b2-w3-c2 | ➡️ | B2 · Semana 3 · Clase 2: Causa, consecuencia, adición y finalidad
desc: Therefore, as a result, consequently, due to, because of, since, furthermore, moreover, in addition, so that, in order to, so as not to.
- ➡️ | Therefore, | Por lo tanto, | The roads were closed. Therefore, we stayed at home. | Las pistas estaban cerradas. Por lo tanto, nos quedamos en casa.
- 📉 | As a result, | Como resultado, | He didn't study. As a result, he failed. | No estudió. Como resultado, desaprobó.
- 🔗 | Consequently, | En consecuencia, | Prices rose. Consequently, sales fell. | Los precios subieron. En consecuencia, las ventas bajaron.
- 🌪️ | due to the storm | debido a la tormenta | The flight was canceled due to the storm. | El vuelo se canceló debido a la tormenta.
- 🚦 | because of the traffic | por el tráfico | We were late because of the traffic. | Llegamos tarde por el tráfico.
- 💡 | Since you're here, help me | Ya que estás aquí, ayúdame | Since you're here, can you help me? | Ya que estás aquí, ¿me ayudas?
- ➕ | Furthermore, | Además, | It's cheap. Furthermore, it's very good quality. | Es barato. Además, es de muy buena calidad.
- ➕ | In addition to this, | Además de esto, | In addition to this, the hotel has a pool. | Además de esto, el hotel tiene piscina.
- 🎯 | in order to save money | para ahorrar dinero | I walk to work in order to save money. | Camino al trabajo para ahorrar dinero.
