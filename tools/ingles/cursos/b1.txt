# ===================== B1 · SEMANA 1: PRESENT PERFECT =====================

=== eng-b1-w1 | 🌍 | B1 · Semana 1 · Clase 1: Present Perfect (experiencias)
desc: Have you ever…? I've been to Cusco. I've never eaten sushi. She has traveled a lot. Experiencias de vida sin fecha.
- 🌍 | Have you ever been to Cusco? | ¿Alguna vez has estado en Cusco? | Have you ever been to Cusco? — Yes, I have. | ¿Alguna vez has estado en Cusco? — Sí.
- ✈️ | I've been to Mexico twice | He estado en México dos veces | I've been to Mexico twice. | He estado en México dos veces.
- 🍣 | I've never eaten sushi | Nunca he comido sushi | I've never eaten sushi. Is it good? | Nunca he comido sushi. ¿Es rico?
- 🧳 | She has traveled a lot | Ella ha viajado mucho | My aunt has traveled a lot. | Mi tía ha viajado mucho.
- 🎬 | Have you seen this movie? | ¿Has visto esta película? | Have you seen this movie? — No, I haven't. | ¿Has visto esta película? — No.
- 🐴 | I've ridden a horse | He montado a caballo | I've ridden a horse in Huaraz. | He montado a caballo en Huaraz.
- 🏅 | We have won three medals | Hemos ganado tres medallas | Our school has won three medals this year. | Nuestro colegio ha ganado tres medallas este año.
- 🎤 | He has never sung in public | Él nunca ha cantado en público | He has never sung in public. He's shy. | Él nunca ha cantado en público. Es tímido.
- 🌊 | Have you ever swum in the sea? | ¿Alguna vez has nadado en el mar? | Have you ever swum in the sea at night? | ¿Alguna vez has nadado en el mar de noche?
- 📕 | I've read that book | He leído ese libro | I've read that book three times. | He leído ese libro tres veces.
- 🤝 | They have met the president | Han conocido al presidente | They have met the president of the club. | Han conocido al presidente del club.
- 🙋 | How many countries have you visited? | ¿Cuántos países has visitado? | How many countries have you visited? — Four. | ¿Cuántos países has visitado? — Cuatro.
- 💯 | It's the best meal I've ever had | Es la mejor comida que he probado | It's the best meal I've ever had! | ¡Es la mejor comida que he probado en mi vida!
teoria:
intro: El Present Perfect une el pasado con el presente. Lo usamos para experiencias de la vida cuando NO decimos cuándo pasó. Se forma con have / has + participio.
tabla: La fórmula ; Persona | Afirmativa* | Negativa* | Pregunta*
| I | I have (I've) worked. | I haven't worked. | Have I worked?
| You | You have (You've) worked. | You haven't worked. | Have you worked?
| He / She / It | She has (She's) worked. | She hasn't worked. | Has she worked?
| We | We have (We've) worked. | We haven't worked. | Have we worked?
| They | They have (They've) worked. | They haven't worked. | Have they worked?
tabla: Respuestas cortas ; Pregunta* | Sí* | No*
| Have you ever been to Puno? | Yes, I have. | No, I haven't.
| Has she ever flown? | Yes, she has. | No, she hasn't.
tabla: Palabras de experiencia ; Inglés* | Español
| ever (en preguntas) | alguna vez
| never | nunca
| once / twice / three times | una vez / dos veces / tres veces
| the best … I've ever … | el mejor … que he … en mi vida
ej: Have you ever tried ceviche? — Yes, many times! | ¿Alguna vez has probado ceviche? — ¡Sí, muchas veces!
ej: I've never been on a plane. | Nunca he subido a un avión.
ojo: Si dices CUÁNDO (yesterday, in 2020, last week) usa el pasado simple: I went to Cusco last year ✓, I have gone to Cusco last year ✗.
ojo: «He estado en» = have been to (no «have gone to», que significa que se fue y aún no vuelve).
ojo: Con never no se usa not: I've never seen it ✓, I haven't never seen it ✗.
pron: I've suena «áiv», she's «shíis», haven't «jávent», hasn't «jásent».

=== eng-b1-w1-c2 | 📜 | B1 · Semana 1 · Clase 2: Participios irregulares
desc: Be–been, do–done, go–gone, see–seen, eat–eaten, write–written, take–taken, give–given, break–broken, fly–flown, swim–swum, drink–drunk, make–made, buy–bought, lose–lost.
- 🌀 | be – was/were – been | ser/estar – estado | I've been very busy. | He estado muy ocupado.
- ✅ | do – did – done | hacer – hecho | Have you done your homework? | ¿Has hecho tu tarea?
- 🚪 | go – went – gone | ir – ido | She has gone to the store. | Ella se ha ido a la tienda.
- 👀 | see – saw – seen | ver – visto | I've seen that movie. | He visto esa película.
- 🍽️ | eat – ate – eaten | comer – comido | Have you eaten anything? | ¿Has comido algo?
- ✍️ | write – wrote – written | escribir – escrito | He has written two books. | Él ha escrito dos libros.
- 📸 | take – took – taken | tomar – tomado | I've taken a lot of photos. | He tomado muchas fotos.
- 🎁 | give – gave – given | dar – dado | They've given me a present. | Me han dado un regalo.
- 💔 | break – broke – broken | romper – roto | I've broken my phone. | He roto mi celular.
- ✈️ | fly – flew – flown | volar – volado | Have you ever flown in a helicopter? | ¿Alguna vez has volado en helicóptero?
- 🏊 | swim – swam – swum | nadar – nadado | I've swum in Lake Titicaca. | He nadado en el lago Titicaca.
- 🥤 | drink – drank – drunk | beber – bebido | Have you drunk chicha morada? | ¿Has tomado chicha morada?
- 🛠️ | make – made – made | hacer – hecho | We've made a big mistake. | Hemos cometido un gran error.
- 🛍️ | buy – bought – bought | comprar – comprado | I've bought the tickets. | He comprado las entradas.
- 😢 | lose – lost – lost | perder – perdido | I've lost my keys again! | ¡He perdido mis llaves otra vez!
- 🗣️ | speak – spoke – spoken | hablar – hablado | Have you spoken to the teacher? | ¿Has hablado con la profesora?
- 🧠 | forget – forgot – forgotten | olvidar – olvidado | I've forgotten his name. | He olvidado su nombre.
- 🎓 | know – knew – known | conocer – conocido | I've known her for years. | La conozco desde hace años.
teoria:
intro: El Present Perfect necesita el participio (tercera columna). En verbos regulares es igual al pasado (-ed); los irregulares hay que aprenderlos. Agruparlos por patrón ayuda mucho.
tabla: Los tres iguales (A-A-A) ; Base* | Pasado* | Participio*
| cut | cut | cut
| put | put | put
| let | let | let
| hit | hit | hit
tabla: Pasado = participio (A-B-B) ; Base* | Pasado* | Participio*
| buy | bought | bought
| make | made | made
| lose | lost | lost
| have | had | had
| find | found | found
| tell | told | told
tabla: Tres formas distintas (A-B-C) ; Base* | Pasado* | Participio*
| be | was / were | been
| do | did | done
| go | went | gone
| see | saw | seen
| eat | ate | eaten
| write | wrote | written
| take | took | taken
| give | gave | given
| break | broke | broken
| speak | spoke | spoken
| forget | forgot | forgotten
| know | knew | known
| fly | flew | flown
tabla: Patrón i – a – u ; Base* | Pasado* | Participio*
| drink | drank | drunk
| swim | swam | swum
| sing | sang | sung
| begin | began | begun
| ring | rang | rung
ej: She has gone to Lima. (todavía está allá) | Ella se ha ido a Lima.
ej: She has been to Lima. (ya volvió) | Ella ha estado en Lima.
ojo: No uses el pasado como participio: I have went ✗ → I have gone ✓. I have saw ✗ → I have seen ✓.
ojo: Been vs gone: been = fue y volvió; gone = se fue y sigue allá.
pron: Been suena «bín», done «dan», gone «gon», written «ríten», known «nóun» (sin k).

=== eng-b1-w1-c3 | ⏳ | B1 · Semana 1 · Clase 3: For, since, already, yet, just
desc: I've lived here for ten years / since 2015. How long have you…? I've already finished. Have you finished yet? I've just arrived.
- 🏠 | I've lived here for ten years | Vivo aquí desde hace diez años | I've lived in Arequipa for ten years. | Vivo en Arequipa desde hace diez años.
- 📅 | I've lived here since 2015 | Vivo aquí desde 2015 | I've lived here since 2015. | Vivo aquí desde 2015.
- ⏱️ | How long have you known her? | ¿Desde cuándo la conoces? | How long have you known her? — Since we were kids. | ¿Desde cuándo la conoces? — Desde niños.
- 🐶 | We've had our dog for two years | Tenemos a nuestro perro hace dos años | We've had our dog for two years. | Tenemos a nuestro perro hace dos años.
- 🎒 | She has been sick since Monday | Ella está enferma desde el lunes | She has been sick since Monday. | Ella está enferma desde el lunes.
- ✅ | I've already finished | Ya terminé | I've already finished my homework. | Ya terminé mi tarea.
- ❓ | Have you finished yet? | ¿Ya terminaste? | Have you finished the book yet? | ¿Ya terminaste el libro?
- ⏳ | I haven't finished yet | Todavía no termino | I haven't finished yet. Give me five minutes. | Todavía no termino. Dame cinco minutos.
- 🚪 | I've just arrived | Acabo de llegar | I've just arrived home. | Acabo de llegar a casa.
- 📩 | She has just called | Ella acaba de llamar | Your mom has just called. | Tu mamá acaba de llamar.
- 🆕 | I haven't seen him lately | No lo he visto últimamente | I haven't seen Carlos lately. | No he visto a Carlos últimamente.
- 🔢 | This is the first time I've tried it | Es la primera vez que lo pruebo | This is the first time I've tried cuy. | Es la primera vez que pruebo cuy.
teoria:
intro: El Present Perfect también sirve para algo que empezó en el pasado y SIGUE ahora (for / since), y para hablar de acciones recientes con already, yet y just.
tabla: For y since ; Palabra* | Se usa con | Ejemplos*
| for | una duración (cuánto tiempo) | for two hours, for a week, for ten years, for a long time
| since | un punto de inicio (desde cuándo) | since Monday, since 2015, since I was a child, since 8 o'clock
tabla: Already, yet, just ; Palabra* | Significa | Dónde va | Ejemplo*
| already | ya | antes del participio | I've already eaten.
| yet | ya (pregunta) / todavía (negativa) | al final | Have you eaten yet? / I haven't eaten yet.
| just | acabar de | antes del participio | I've just eaten.
tabla: Pregunta por la duración ; Pregunta* | Respuesta*
| How long have you lived here? | For five years. / Since 2020.
| How long have you had your phone? | For six months.
ej: I've studied English since I was eight. | Estudio inglés desde que tenía ocho años.
ej: The movie has just started. | La película acaba de empezar.
ojo: «Vivo aquí desde hace 5 años» NO es presente en inglés: I live here for 5 years ✗ → I've lived here for 5 years ✓.
ojo: «Desde hace» = for; «desde» = since. Since three years ✗ → for three years ✓.
ojo: «Acabo de llegar» = I've just arrived (no «I finish of arrive»).
pron: Already suena «olrédi». Yet suena «iét». Since suena «sins».

=== eng-b1-w1-c4 | ⚖️ | B1 · Semana 1 · Clase 4: Present Perfect vs Past Simple
desc: I've been to Cusco (experiencia) vs I went to Cusco last year (fecha). Have you ever…? — Yes. When did you go?
- 🌍 | I've been to Cusco | He estado en Cusco | I've been to Cusco. It's beautiful. | He estado en Cusco. Es hermoso.
- 📅 | I went to Cusco last year | Fui a Cusco el año pasado | I went to Cusco last year with my class. | Fui a Cusco el año pasado con mi clase.
- ❓ | When did you go? | ¿Cuándo fuiste? | Have you been to Ica? — Yes. — When did you go? | ¿Has estado en Ica? — Sí. — ¿Cuándo fuiste?
- 🔑 | I've lost my keys | He perdido mis llaves (aún no las tengo) | I've lost my keys. Can you help me? | He perdido mis llaves. ¿Me ayudas?
- 🗝️ | I lost my keys yesterday | Perdí mis llaves ayer | I lost my keys yesterday, but I found them. | Ayer perdí mis llaves, pero las encontré.
- 📚 | I've read five books this month | He leído cinco libros este mes | I've read five books this month. | He leído cinco libros este mes.
- 📖 | I read five books last month | Leí cinco libros el mes pasado | I read five books last month. | Leí cinco libros el mes pasado.
- 🏫 | She has worked here since 2018 | Ella trabaja aquí desde 2018 | She has worked here since 2018. | Ella trabaja aquí desde 2018.
- 🏢 | She worked there for two years | Trabajó allí dos años | She worked there for two years and then left. | Trabajó allí dos años y luego se fue.
- 🎨 | Picasso painted many pictures | Picasso pintó muchos cuadros | Picasso painted many pictures. | Picasso pintó muchos cuadros.
- 👩‍🎨 | My sister has painted three pictures | Mi hermana ha pintado tres cuadros | My sister has painted three pictures this year. | Mi hermana ha pintado tres cuadros este año.
- 🕐 | What time did you arrive? | ¿A qué hora llegaste? | What time did you arrive? — At nine. | ¿A qué hora llegaste? — A las nueve.
teoria:
intro: Los hispanohablantes confundimos mucho estos dos tiempos. La clave: ¿dices cuándo pasó? ¿el tiempo ya terminó? Entonces es Past Simple.
tabla: ¿Cuál uso? ; Present Perfect* | Past Simple*
| I've visited Cusco. (alguna vez) | I visited Cusco in 2022. (fecha)
| I've lost my phone. (todavía no aparece) | I lost my phone, but I found it.
| I've worked here since 2020. (sigue) | I worked there for two years. (ya no)
| I've seen him today. (hoy no termina) | I saw him yesterday.
tabla: Palabras guía ; Present Perfect* | Past Simple*
| ever, never | yesterday
| already, yet, just | last week / last year
| for, since (hasta ahora) | ago
| today, this week, this year | in 2019, when I was a child
ej: Have you ever eaten ceviche? — Yes, I have. I ate it last Sunday. | ¿Has comido ceviche? — Sí. Lo comí el domingo pasado.
ej: I've known Ana since 2019. We met at school. | Conozco a Ana desde 2019. Nos conocimos en el colegio.
ojo: Una conversación suele empezar en Present Perfect (Have you ever…?) y seguir en Past Simple con los detalles (When did you…? How was it?).
ojo: «Ago» siempre va con pasado simple: I've arrived two hours ago ✗ → I arrived two hours ago ✓.
pron: Escucha la diferencia: «ai víziti» (I visited) y «aiv víziti» (I've visited): la «v» es corta pero importante.

# ===================== B1 · SEMANA 2: NARRAR EN PASADO =====================

=== eng-b1-w2 | 🎞️ | B1 · Semana 2 · Clase 1: Past Continuous
desc: I was studying at 8 p.m. They were playing. What were you doing? It was raining. Todas las personas, negativa y pregunta.
- 📚 | I was studying at eight | Estaba estudiando a las ocho | At eight o'clock I was studying. | A las ocho estaba estudiando.
- ⚽ | They were playing soccer | Estaban jugando fútbol | At four they were playing soccer. | A las cuatro estaban jugando fútbol.
- 🌧️ | It was raining | Estaba lloviendo | It was raining all morning. | Estuvo lloviendo toda la mañana.
- ❓ | What were you doing? | ¿Qué estabas haciendo? | What were you doing last night at ten? | ¿Qué estabas haciendo anoche a las diez?
- 😴 | I was sleeping | Estaba durmiendo | Sorry, I didn't answer. I was sleeping. | Perdón, no contesté. Estaba durmiendo.
- 🍳 | Mom was cooking | Mamá estaba cocinando | Mom was cooking and Dad was cleaning. | Mamá estaba cocinando y papá estaba limpiando.
- 🚫 | We weren't watching TV | No estábamos viendo televisión | We weren't watching TV. We were reading. | No estábamos viendo tele. Estábamos leyendo.
- 🙅 | She wasn't listening | Ella no estaba escuchando | She wasn't listening to the teacher. | Ella no estaba escuchando a la profesora.
- ❔ | Were you sleeping? | ¿Estabas durmiendo? | Were you sleeping? — No, I wasn't. | ¿Estabas durmiendo? — No.
- 🌅 | The sun was shining | El sol estaba brillando | The sun was shining and the birds were singing. | El sol brillaba y los pájaros cantaban.
- 🚶 | People were walking in the park | La gente caminaba en el parque | Many people were walking in the park. | Mucha gente caminaba en el parque.
- 🎧 | He was listening to music all afternoon | Estuvo escuchando música toda la tarde | He was listening to music all afternoon. | Estuvo escuchando música toda la tarde.
teoria:
intro: El Past Continuous describe una acción que estaba en progreso en un momento del pasado. Sirve para dar el «fondo» o escenario de una historia.
tabla: La fórmula ; Persona | Afirmativa* | Negativa* | Pregunta*
| I | I was working. | I wasn't working. | Was I working?
| You | You were working. | You weren't working. | Were you working?
| He / She / It | She was working. | She wasn't working. | Was she working?
| We | We were working. | We weren't working. | Were we working?
| They | They were working. | They weren't working. | Were they working?
tabla: Respuestas cortas ; Pregunta* | Sí* | No*
| Were you studying? | Yes, I was. | No, I wasn't.
| Was it raining? | Yes, it was. | No, it wasn't.
| Were they playing? | Yes, they were. | No, they weren't.
ej: At midnight I was still doing my homework. | A medianoche todavía estaba haciendo mi tarea.
ej: It was a cold night. The wind was blowing. | Era una noche fría. El viento soplaba.
ojo: I / he / she / it → was. You / we / they → were. They was ✗ → They were ✓.
ojo: Los verbos de estado (know, like, want) no van en -ing: I was knowing ✗ → I knew ✓.
pron: Was suena «uos» (débil: «uəs»). Weren't suena «uérent».

=== eng-b1-w2-c2 | 📖 | B1 · Semana 2 · Clase 2: When / while: contar una historia
desc: I was walking home when it started to rain. While I was cooking, the phone rang. Escenario + acción que interrumpe.
- 🌧️ | I was walking home when it started to rain | Caminaba a casa cuando empezó a llover | I was walking home when it started to rain. | Caminaba a casa cuando empezó a llover.
- 📞 | While I was cooking, the phone rang | Mientras cocinaba, sonó el teléfono | While I was cooking, the phone rang. | Mientras cocinaba, sonó el teléfono.
- 💡 | The lights went out while we were watching a movie | Se fue la luz mientras veíamos una película | The lights went out while we were watching a movie. | Se fue la luz mientras veíamos una película.
- 🤕 | She fell while she was running | Se cayó mientras corría | She fell while she was running in the park. | Se cayó mientras corría en el parque.
- 🐕 | When I opened the door, the dog ran out | Cuando abrí la puerta, el perro salió corriendo | When I opened the door, the dog ran out. | Cuando abrí la puerta, el perro salió corriendo.
- 👋 | I saw my friend while I was waiting for the bus | Vi a mi amigo mientras esperaba el bus | I saw my friend while I was waiting for the bus. | Vi a mi amigo mientras esperaba el bus.
- 🎂 | We were having dinner when they arrived | Estábamos cenando cuando llegaron | We were having dinner when they arrived. | Estábamos cenando cuando llegaron.
- 🔊 | Suddenly, I heard a noise | De repente, escuché un ruido | Suddenly, I heard a strange noise. | De repente, escuché un ruido extraño.
- 🌙 | It was a dark and stormy night | Era una noche oscura y tormentosa | It was a dark and stormy night. | Era una noche oscura y tormentosa.
- 🎉 | In the end, everything was fine | Al final, todo salió bien | In the end, everything was fine. | Al final, todo salió bien.
- ⏰ | At that moment, the teacher came in | En ese momento, entró la profesora | At that moment, the teacher came in. | En ese momento, entró la profesora.
- 🧭 | One day, I was walking in the forest | Un día, caminaba por el bosque | One day, I was walking in the forest… | Un día, caminaba por el bosque…
teoria:
intro: Para narrar combinamos Past Continuous (la acción larga, el escenario) y Past Simple (la acción corta que interrumpe). When y while las conectan.
tabla: Acción larga + acción corta ; Estructura | Ejemplo*
| Past Continuous + when + Past Simple | I was sleeping when the alarm rang.
| While + Past Continuous, Past Simple | While I was sleeping, the alarm rang.
| Dos acciones largas a la vez | While I was cooking, my sister was setting the table.
| Acciones en secuencia (todas Past Simple) | When she arrived, we started to eat.
tabla: Palabras para narrar ; Inglés* | Español
| One day… | Un día…
| It was a … day/night | Era un día / una noche …
| Suddenly… | De repente…
| At that moment… | En ese momento…
| Then… / After that… | Luego… / Después de eso…
| In the end… / Finally… | Al final… / Finalmente…
ej: While we were playing, it started to rain, so we went inside. | Mientras jugábamos, empezó a llover, así que entramos.
ej: When the teacher came in, everybody was talking. | Cuando entró la profesora, todos estaban hablando.
ojo: «While» va con la acción larga (-ing); «when» suele ir con la acción corta.
ojo: Cambia el significado: When he arrived, we were eating (ya comíamos) vs When he arrived, we ate (comimos después).
pron: Suddenly suena «sádenli». While suena «uáil».

=== eng-b1-w2-c3 | 🕰️ | B1 · Semana 2 · Clase 3: Used to (costumbres del pasado)
desc: I used to play with dolls. I didn't use to like vegetables. Did you use to…? Hábitos que ya no existen.
- 🧸 | I used to play with dolls | Antes jugaba con muñecas | When I was little, I used to play with dolls. | Cuando era pequeña, jugaba con muñecas.
- 🥦 | I didn't use to like vegetables | Antes no me gustaban las verduras | I didn't use to like vegetables, but now I love them. | Antes no me gustaban las verduras, pero ahora me encantan.
- ❓ | Did you use to live here? | ¿Antes vivías aquí? | Did you use to live in Trujillo? — Yes, I did. | ¿Antes vivías en Trujillo? — Sí.
- 👓 | He used to wear glasses | Él antes usaba lentes | He used to wear glasses, but now he wears contacts. | Él antes usaba lentes, pero ahora usa lentes de contacto.
- 🏡 | We used to live in the countryside | Antes vivíamos en el campo | We used to live in the countryside. | Antes vivíamos en el campo.
- 📺 | There used to be a cinema here | Antes había un cine aquí | There used to be a cinema on this corner. | Antes había un cine en esta esquina.
- 🚲 | My dad used to ride his bike to work | Mi papá antes iba en bici al trabajo | My dad used to ride his bike to work. | Mi papá antes iba en bici al trabajo.
- 💇 | She used to have long hair | Ella antes tenía el pelo largo | She used to have long hair. | Ella antes tenía el pelo largo.
- 😨 | I used to be afraid of the dark | Antes le tenía miedo a la oscuridad | I used to be afraid of the dark. | Antes le tenía miedo a la oscuridad.
- 🍬 | We used to eat candy every day | Antes comíamos dulces todos los días | We used to eat candy every day. | Antes comíamos dulces todos los días.
- 📞 | People used to write letters | La gente antes escribía cartas | People used to write letters. Now they send messages. | La gente antes escribía cartas. Ahora manda mensajes.
teoria:
intro: «Used to» habla de costumbres o situaciones del pasado que YA NO pasan. En español solemos decir «antes + imperfecto» (antes jugaba, antes vivía).
tabla: Las tres formas ; Forma | Estructura | Ejemplo*
| Afirmativa | used to + verbo | I used to play.
| Negativa | didn't use to + verbo | I didn't use to play.
| Pregunta | Did … use to + verbo? | Did you use to play?
tabla: Used to vs pasado simple ; Used to* | Past Simple*
| I used to go to the beach every summer. (costumbre) | I went to the beach last summer. (una vez)
| She used to live in Tacna. (por años) | She lived in Tacna for two years.
ej: When my grandpa was young, he used to walk to school. | Cuando mi abuelo era joven, caminaba al colegio.
ej: Did you use to watch cartoons? — Yes, every morning! | ¿Antes veías dibujos animados? — ¡Sí, todas las mañanas!
ojo: En negativa y pregunta se escribe «use to» (sin d): I didn't used to ✗ → I didn't use to ✓.
ojo: Para el presente no se usa: I use to get up early ✗ → I usually get up early ✓.
pron: Used to suena «iústu» (la «d» no suena y la «s» es una s, no una z).

# ===================== B1 · SEMANA 3: FUTURO =====================

=== eng-b1-w3 | 🔮 | B1 · Semana 3 · Clase 1: Will (decisiones, promesas, predicciones)
desc: I'll help you. I won't tell anyone. I think it will rain. Will you marry me? Shall I open the window?
